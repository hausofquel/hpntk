What HPNTK means

HPNTK is a creative studio exploring digital art, motion, and experimental media — dedicated to studying altered states of perception and building a visual language for the parts of identity and experience that resist a single, fixed image. The medium may change, but the purpose stays the same: inviting anyone who encounters the work to look longer, question what's real, and find their own reflection in the distortion.
